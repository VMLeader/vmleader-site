Presented live by Tom Cox, Executive Coach & 3x CEO

The workforce is a different place than it was 10 years ago. AI. Layoffs. Reorgs. Return to Office. All of these mean your W2 paycheck can vanish overnight, which means having one job stopped being the safe bet.

In this 90-minute Live Masterclass, Tom Cox shows how to turn 10 to 20+ years of corporate expertise into income you can switch on whenever you want. A layoff becomes a pivot. AI becomes a tool you wield yourself. And you move between corporate roles and your own thing, on your terms.

Who this is for

For the entrepreneur-curious professional or executive.

You are good at what you do. You still love your work. What you want is more impact, more freedom, and income that doesn't depend on one employer.

If you have caught yourself daydreaming about your own coaching, consulting, or fractional practice, this session was built for you.

And if you have ever wanted to build leverage in your career to move upward, this is also for you.

What you will learn

Three shifts that make diversifying your income the safe move.

We skip the busywork of logos and websites and focus on the only thing that creates real independence: validated income.

Shift #1

Reposition your expertise

Your 10 to 20+ years are a product you can sell. Learn to package that expertise into a high-value offer people will pay for, with no logo, website, or year of building required.

Shift #2

The Revenue First Framework™

Tom's method to skip the busywork and go straight to real market conversations and land your first paying client, without ever selling in a way that feels slimy or uncomfortable.

Shift #3

The no-risk, high-reward plan

Land a paying client while you keep your job, so you start building an income machine before risking your mortgage payment. You do not resign until the income is real.

The Outdated Way
Why good people stay stuck

Three reasons smart leaders stay in a job they have outgrown.

Conditioned to depend on a paycheck

We were taught our whole lives that one employer equals security. That is NOT how the world works in 2027 and beyond.

The fear of failure

When you are the breadwinner, the risk of failing is scary, not just to you but to your family as well. So you play it safe, which ironically is now the more dangerous move.

No clear roadmap

Wanting to build your own thing is great. Knowing HOW to do it without risk is where most people get stuck.

The biggest risk for a professional or executive in 2027 is a single source of income.

This masterclass shows you how to become a free agent in your career, with multiple income sources.
