The workforce is a different place than it was 10 years ago. AI. Layoffs. Reorgs. Return to Office. All of these mean your W2 paycheck can vanish overnight, which means having one job stopped being the safe bet.

In this 90-minute Live Masterclass, Tom Cox shows how to turn 10 to 20+ years of corporate expertise into a practice you can turn up or down whenever you need it. A layoff becomes a pivot. A promotion becomes something you finally ask for. AI becomes a tool you wield yourself, and you move between corporate roles and your own thing, on your terms.

Which one are you?

Three people come to this session. Same practice, three modes.

The mechanism is the same for all three. Only the dial moves.

Standby Income

You want to stay and grow

You like the work. You want the promotion, the scope, and the say, and you want to stop being afraid in the room. You would rather have options than an exit.

Bridge Income

You are between roles

Interviews, second rounds, ghosting and no thank you's. You want income and dignity while the search runs, and a reason the next interview feels different.

Full Income

You are ready to build

You have circled the same three options for a year. Consulting, coaching, fractional. You want out without gambling the mortgage, and someone to show you the order to do it in.

Why good people stay stuck

Three reasons smart leaders stay in a job they have outgrown.

Conditioned to depend on a paycheck

We were taught our whole lives that one employer equals security. That story is getting more expensive every year.

The fear of failing

When you are the breadwinner, the downside feels too big to touch, so you keep waiting for a safer time that never comes.

No map

Wanting out, or wanting more, is easy. Knowing the first move, and the one after that, is where almost everyone gets stuck.
