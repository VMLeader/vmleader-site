The mechanism

One practice, built once. The Income Dial does the rest.

You build one practice from expertise you already have, then turn it to whichever mode your life needs. Same offer, same clients. Only the dial moves. And once it is built, the dial never goes back to off.

Mode One

Standby Income

Employed. The practice idles warm, an offer and a client or two, a few hours a month. You walk into the review, and into the interview, with a number in your pocket.

Mode Two

Bridge Income

Between roles. You turn it up to fill the gap. Severance becomes runway, and "I'm consulting" changes who is asking the questions.

Mode Three

Full Income

All the way up. You resign when you hit your Freedom Number, not before. And you can always turn the dial back and go W2 again.

Your Freedom Number is the gauge on the dial: the monthly income your practice needs for the mode you want. One client at $5K changes how you show up at work.
